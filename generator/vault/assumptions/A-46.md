## Verdict note

The added fabric's U-VALUES are DEAP Table S2's band L row - new work meets the current regulations, which is a regulatory fact rather than a choice. The AREA is declared. PxStat publishes planning permissions for extensions as COUNTS and new-dwelling floor areas (and TRS05, declared, for rental floor area), but nothing that gives the size of an extension to an existing home. Recorded with its direction of effect measured rather than argued: an extension both adds envelope and dilutes specific loss, and the decade's thirteen extensions came out at HLC ratios 1.051 to 1.080 in v1/evidence/wp5_tenyear.json.
