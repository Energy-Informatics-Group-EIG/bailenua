## Verdict note

The earlier verdict was right about migration ESTIMATES and wrong about where else to look. EP006 publishes NET migration per 1,000 by county and PEB08 projects it; neither gives a gross flow, and a net rate of +0.6 per 1,000 (Cork, 2011-2016) is consistent with any pair of gross flows differing by that much, including zero and zero. All of that still holds. What it missed is that the quantity is also a CENSUS QUESTION. Every resident is asked where they usually lived one year before Census Day, and SAP2022 publishes the answer at TOWN level - so for Cobh itself, not for Cork County and not for the South-West.
