## Verdict note

The CSO publishes which fuel households cook WITH (A-33, now retired) and not how efficiently the appliance uses it, and DEAP is silent because cooking is outside its primary-energy boundary - which data/ber_bands.yaml already records. But the gas METER sees cooking, because DBERNG01 is a meter reading, so it cannot be left out. The assumption is kept as narrow as it can be: the model re-delivers the useful cooking energy the appliance layer has already sized, at a burner's efficiency, so what is declared is a single RATIO rather than a new annual energy invented from nothing. A flame loses heat around the pan and a gas oven vents its combustion products; the direction is not in doubt.
