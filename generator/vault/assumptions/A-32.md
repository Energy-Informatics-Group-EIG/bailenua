## Verdict note

The single assumption the whole hot water volume turns on, and the one that went wrong first. DEAP publishes the FLOW RATE - a minimum of 6 L/min on every mixer shower - and takes the DURATION as an assessor input, so there is no default to use. The activity engine gives time in the bathroom, which is a different quantity: charging 6 L/min across a 25-minute bathroom episode made 150-litre showers and about 800 litres a day per household. Bounded from two sides rather than fitted - PL-DHW-01 against DEAP's per-person water target above, and the gas meter below, since DBERNG01 contains hot water and the meter comparison comes in at 0.841 of the published cell rather than over it.
