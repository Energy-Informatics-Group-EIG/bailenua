## Verdict note

The U-VALUE is DEAP's, not assumed: Table S4 publishes the roof U-value for each observed insulation thickness, and at >=300 mm it is 0.13 W/m2K. Only the DEPTH is declared, and it is declared at the grant standard rather than chosen - the alternative depths DEAP publishes were not swept for the one that moved U31, which D-072 forbids. PxStat carries SEAI grant COUNTS by scheme and year, which say how many homes were insulated and not to what depth; the depth is an installation specification, not a statistic.
