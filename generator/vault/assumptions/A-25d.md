## Verdict note

EA036 publishes a COUNT of persons aged 20 still living with parents, for 2011 and 2016 only, at that single age - 37,995 in 2016. One age in two years cannot be differenced into a hazard over 18 to 34. 'leaving home' returns 82 tables, all of which are the census TIME LEAVING HOME question - what time of the morning people leave for work - which is a different thing entirely and is a good example of why a hit count is not evidence. EA036 is used as a weak bound: even its denominator is approximate, because PEA01's finest band is 20-24.
