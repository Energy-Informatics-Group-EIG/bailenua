## Verdict note

This is a codelist mapping between two HETUS activity codes, not a statistic. PxStat publishes carer COUNTS (census tables on persons providing unpaid care) but no duration that could be substituted into a semi-Markov duration distribution. The census carer counts constrain WHO cares, which the population layer already takes from SAPS, not HOW LONG, which is the assumption.
