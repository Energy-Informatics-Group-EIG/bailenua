## Verdict note

The MAGNITUDE is DEAP's and the branch is the assumption, which is the opposite way round from most entries here and worth saying: Table 4c(4) publishes 1.0 for underfloor or warm air, 0.85 for fan-coil or low-temperature radiators, 0.70 for standard radiators without compensation and 0.75 with. Nothing is invented; the question is which published row a converted dwelling belongs in. Declared at 0.70, which is DEAP's WORST branch, and deliberately so: of the four it is the only one that cannot flatter the measure, and it is the physically right answer for the measure WP5 actually models - a fabric retrofit does not resize radiators.
