## Verdict note

KEPT, and it is a vintage problem rather than an unsourced one. A three-band domestic plan with published day, night and peak bands was not on sale in May 2022 - the national smart meter rollout had not reached the point where suppliers published one - so there is no contemporaneous schedule to fetch. The flat and day/night plans ARE contemporaneous (1 May 2022, the simulated year). The consequence is specific and bounded: one of three plans carries 2024 price levels, which are higher, so a comparison ACROSS plans in this dataset mixes two years and a comparison WITHIN a plan does not.
