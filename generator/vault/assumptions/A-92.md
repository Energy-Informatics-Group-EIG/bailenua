## Verdict note

KEPT, and narrowed to one thing. The three-band plan's hours ARE published and are used as published (day 08:00-23:00 excluding peak, night 23:00-08:00, peak 17:00-19:00, CRU-regulated supplier schedule, page 3). What is not published anywhere in the declared corpus is the NightSaver meter's own window: the 2022 price schedule states the rates and not the hours, and PxStat returns zero hits for all four search terms over 13,086 tables because a meter switching time is a term of a connection and not a statistic. DEAP Appendix F (p.104) does establish the SHAPE - one off-peak period each night - so a single contiguous night band is sourced even where its boundaries are carried.
