## Verdict note

Declared and deliberately NOT fitted. DBERNG01 is the declared calibration axis so fitting would have been permitted, but the mechanism saturates at a 45% BER spread against a 4-7% target (I-048), so a fitted strength would buy the appearance of calibration and a kelvin of indoor temperature and nothing else. The sweep is published beside the declared values instead, in `v1/evidence/ablation_u22.json`, so a reader can see what each strength costs. Zero hits for indoor temperature confirms there is no Irish measurement to anchor the consequence against either (A-09).
