# A-42 — electricity outage frequency and duration

| | |
|---|---|
| Verdict | WRONG_INSTRUMENT |
| Assumed | a quarter of storm days produce a local outage, lasting 1 to 6 hours |
| Retires if | a published Irish supply-interruption statistic - customer minutes lost, customer interruptions, or an outage duration distribution |

## Verdict note

Zero tables. Supply reliability is published by the regulator and the network operator, not the statistics office - the same split that put the standard load profiles at ESB Networks rather than in PxStat (I-043). What makes this assumption narrow is that only the CONDITIONAL probability is assumed: outages are generated FROM storm days, and the storm days come from the weather realisation, so the outage RATE inherits a sourced frequency. An outage drawn independently would put the lights out on a calm day, and a model that had learned the real storm-outage correlation would be penalised for being right.
