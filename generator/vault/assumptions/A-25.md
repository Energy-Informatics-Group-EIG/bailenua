Week 2 step 5 wired all of these in, so the flag is gone for the hazards it covered. Births come from VSA104's age-specific fertility rates by NUTS 3 region (South-West), split by parity from VSA36. Partnering comes from VSA49 by single year of age. Employment change comes from JCA01, which publishes job churn - hirings and separations - by single year of age and sex; the census labour-force tables are stocks and give no transition rate at all.
