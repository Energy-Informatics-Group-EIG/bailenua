## Verdict note

Twelve hits for `boiler`, and every one is about the FUEL burned in an open fire or stove with a back boiler (HEBEU13-24). The CSO publishes what households heat WITH - HEBEU06 and HEBEU103-108, main fuel or energy source used to heat the home, now declared for the cooking derivation - and nothing about how old the appliance is. DEAP takes the boiler from the assessment rather than modelling its replacement. The DIRECTION is not in doubt: boilers are replaced and dwellings are not, which is the whole of I-047. The consequence of the exact figure being wrong is bounded, because any service life under about 45 years puts a pre-1980 dwelling on a post-1998 boiler, which is the Table 4b row …
