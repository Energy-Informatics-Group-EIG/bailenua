## Verdict note

The half of I-039 that LP1 does not close, and the distinction matters: LP1 is the aggregate of a whole customer class, which is the right instrument for feeder loading and the wrong one for one dwelling. A single household's peak is a kettle and a shower coinciding, and measuring it needs smart-meter or sub-metered microdata. In Ireland that means CER/ISSDA, which the brief forbids, so this is not a gap a search can close. Recorded so that PL-PEAK-01's after-diversity result is never read as a per-dwelling one.
