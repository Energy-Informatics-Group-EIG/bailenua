| Retires if | published Irish rates for acute illness episodes, remote-working days, appliance failures or household visitors |

## Verdict note

These were covered by A-25 until week 2 step 5 narrowed A-25 to the life-course hazards it had actually sourced, which left the disruption rates without an entry of their own - caught by uncovered_files(), which is what that check is for. They are a different kind of quantity from a life-course hazard: a disruption is a short perturbation to a day, and design 5.3's acceptance test (R17) is that rates fall within cited ranges rather than that they match a national statistic. The illness rate already carries its own correction: an earlier version added household contagion on top of a survey total that already included it, producing 32.5 illness days per person-year against a real 8 to 12.
