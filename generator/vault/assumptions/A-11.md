## Verdict note

The census counts rooms and never measures them. The only floor areas the CSO publishes are TRS05 (total floor area of rental properties by local authority) and NDA08 (new dwelling size by type), both already declared and neither crossed with room count, so neither gives area PER ROOM. F2063 retires the room-count half of I-019 (A-10) and leaves this half assumed - which is now a precise statement of the gap rather than a general one.
