## Verdict note

RETIRED. ESB Networks' Basis of Charges for Connection to the Distribution System states that 'Domestic connections are only available for the standard capacities of 3kVA, 12kVA, 16kVA, 20kVA and 29kVA' and treats 12 kVA as the standard domestic connection, with 30 kVA or more a business connection. The schema declares the field in kW and the document publishes kVA; the card carries 12.0 and states the unity-power-factor conversion rather than leaving it implicit. PxStat returns zero hits for all three terms, which was correct - the connection agreement was the right instrument.
