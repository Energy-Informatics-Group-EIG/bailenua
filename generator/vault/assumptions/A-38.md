## Verdict note

A-01's UK-donor flag, inherited by a specific parameter - but the parameter improved under it. Before U32 the bedtime spread was INVENTED: a standard deviation of 35 minutes on the sleep duration, giving a 10-to-90 range of 106 minutes and no right tail, which is why nobody in the town was ever awake at 01:00. It is now MEASURED from HETUS UK's share asleep by time of day (code AC0A): a 187-minute range with a tail past 02:50. So the flag stays because Ireland is in neither HETUS wave (I-008, A-01) and the donor is still the UK, not because the number is still a guess. What is transferred is a DISPERSION and not a level: the wake median stays anchored to Cobh's Census travel-to-work tables …
