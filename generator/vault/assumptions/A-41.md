## Verdict note

SCA42 and SCA31 publish chronic absence - the share of children absent TWENTY DAYS OR MORE in a school year - which is not a daily rate but does put a floor under one: 21.8 per cent of Cork primary children missing at least 20 of about 183 days gives 0.0238 from chronic absence alone, before any short absence, and the assumed 0.07 sits above that floor. UA16 'Enrolments by week' was fetched and DISCARDED: its weekly series turned out to be 15 irregular snapshot dates across several years, not an attendance series, so it cannot be differenced into a term calendar. Term DATES remain a transcription from Department of Education circulars, which are documents rather than statistics.
