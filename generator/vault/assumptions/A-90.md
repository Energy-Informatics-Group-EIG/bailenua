## Verdict note

RETIRED. Electric Ireland's Standard Electricity Price Plan dated 1 May 2022 publishes the 24-hour rate (28.12 c/kWh incl. VAT), the NightSaver day and night rates (30.04 and 14.82) and the urban standing charges, and the Home Electric+ Standard Smart Tariff schedule publishes the three-band rates. Both are fetched, hashed and declared. CSO TMEGB03's published band-DC price for 2022 brackets the supplier's figure independently (0.2324 in the first half of the year, 0.3225 in the second, against the schedule's 0.2812), which is what makes the level a measurement rather than a transcription.
