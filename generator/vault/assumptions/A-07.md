## Verdict note

HS219 publishes ownership by household size, not use. Owning a kettle and boiling it four times a day are different quantities, and only the first is published. Sub-metered use frequency is what would settle this, and the only Irish sub-metered trial is CER/ISSDA, which the brief forbids.
