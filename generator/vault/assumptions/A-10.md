## Verdict note

F2063 is the table whose absence caused I-031. Census 2022 publishes rooms x bedrooms x year built x dwelling type x county - the exact four-way joint that was drawn independently and produced a 285.7 m2 three-bedroom apartment burning 13.6 MWh. SAPS publishes these as separate marginals, which is why the assumption looked unavoidable; the county-level census table publishes the joint. Scored by the new PL-ROOMS-01 at Cork County, not used to recalibrate: the geometry was fixed before this was found, so the table judges it rather than fitting it.
