## Decision

An activity whose HETUS daily target is below its own episode floor is spent as an occurrence probability p = target / drawn_episode_length rather than as a daily budget

## Reason

I-028. Such a target does not mean a few minutes every day — sport at 6 minutes a day is 20 minutes twice a week. Treating it as a daily budget gave a 2-minute daily sliver of sport before the episode floor and, after the floor, excluded the activity for ever, so no washing machine in the estate ever ran. The form is mean-preserving by construction
