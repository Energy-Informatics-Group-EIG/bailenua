## Decision

A vacant dwelling stays in the slice with an empty household and is held at frost protection, rather than being dropped

## Reason

WP5's ten-year life course produces vacancies. Dropping the building would take a connected house out of the feeder aggregate, and the move-out label's own claim - that load falls to a standing baseline - would then describe a premises that is not in the data. The runner raised IndexError on people[0] for an empty household, which is the right failure for a state it had never been told could exist. 7 C is [A] (A-40) and deliberately narrow: DEAP has no vacancy case, and the quantity it moves is a standing baseline rather than a peak
