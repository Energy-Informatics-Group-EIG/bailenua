# D-056 — Sleep timing takes its spread from HETUS UK and its medians from Irish sources;

## Decision

Sleep timing takes its spread from HETUS UK and its medians from Irish sources; where the two meet, the Irish anchor bounds the UK one

## Reason

The wake median is anchored to Cobh's Census travel-to-work tables and the commute to its departure bands, so taking the centre from a UK donor would overwrite an Irish measurement with a foreign one. Dispersion transfers between countries far better than when people get up. And the direction of binding matters: a model in which 1% of Cobh's workers wake at 12:40 would be using HETUS to overrule SAPS, so the Census-derived cap damps the UK spread by a derived 0.732 rather than clipping it - clipping piled 16.9% of workers onto a single minute
