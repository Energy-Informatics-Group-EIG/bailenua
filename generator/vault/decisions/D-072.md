## Decision

Three G-C acceptance criteria were wrong before they were right, and each was corrected by the data rather than by argument

## Reason

The non-identifiable mode used r = 0.97 while claiming non-identifiability "at any sample size": false, because variance inflation there is only about 17 and the coefficients come back with standard errors near 0.054. Its criterion then went through a fixed standard-error threshold and half the coefficient gap before arriving at the textbook statement - under collinearity prediction is identified and coefficient inference is not. And the cluster mode compared a silhouette against a declared 0.25, which the data refused: k-means on one correlated Gaussian scores 0.31, because splitting any blob in two gives a respectable silhouette. It now compares against the same statistic on Gaussian data …
