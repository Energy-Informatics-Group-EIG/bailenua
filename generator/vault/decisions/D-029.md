## Decision

Where the DER adoption draw yields none of a type U23 requires, the adoption is assigned to the highest-propensity home under the same model and the assignment is recorded in der_guarantees

## Reason

PV, battery and EV adoption are stochastic, so a 50-home slice can legitimately draw zero batteries (p=0.35 of PV homes). U23 requires at least one because the slice exists to exercise those paths. The model's rates are unchanged and the town-wide draw is untouched — this is a declared guarantee on the slice, not a correction to the model. In the event none was needed: the slice drew 6 PV, 1 battery and 6 EVs
