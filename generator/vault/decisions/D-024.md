# D-024 — Any series the solver uses is carried on its result and consumed by the writer, never recomputed downstream;

## Decision

Any series the solver uses is carried on its result and consumed by the writer, never recomputed downstream; the solver reports the air change rate it actually applied

## Reason

I-032. The writer recomputed D16's solar_gain_w from the weather, so once the solver moved to the glazing plane the published energy balance would have described a different run from the one that happened. The same duplication was about to be repeated for the purge air change rate
