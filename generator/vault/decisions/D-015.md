## Decision

DBEREL01 (dwelling type × BER band) is declared with model_development_exposure="tuning" and DBEREL02 (dwelling type × period of construction) with "none", and the D24 reader raises if DBEREL02's declaration ever changes

## Reason

I-027 and design 7.1. The appliance trigger rates were fitted to DBEREL01, so it cannot also measure whether they generalise. DBEREL02 crosses the same dwellings on an axis the fitting never saw, and D02 already carries year_built_band. A held-out target that has quietly been used for tuning is worse than no held-out target, because it supports a claim that is not true
