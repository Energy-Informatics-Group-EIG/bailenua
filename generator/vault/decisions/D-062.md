## Decision

Fabric retrofit and extension produce their D03 passport events and D20 labels but do not change the RC parameters in week 2

## Reason

I-048 defers the fabric work to week 3 and the standing instruction is no fabric tuning now. Suppressing the passport events instead would make the ten-year building history wrong in order to make a week-2 package self-consistent - the events are ground truth about what happened to the building. The consequence is real and recorded as I-054: a detector scored against a retrofit_fabric label in a week-2 package scores a false negative through no fault of its own, for 8 fabric retrofits and 10 extensions. It is in the run diagnostics and asserted by a test, so week 3 has to come back and flip it
