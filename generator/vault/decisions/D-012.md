## Decision

Joint and spatial fidelity are measured as between-small-area quantities (Spearman association across areas, Moran's I, dissimilarity index), and every report says so

## Reason

SAPS publishes no individual-level cross-tabulation, so there is no joint distribution to compare against. Claiming individual-level joint validation would be unfalsifiable, which design 7 forbids
