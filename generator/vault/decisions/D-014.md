## Decision

Shared appliances are triggered once per household episode — a maximal run of overlapping intervals of the same activity in the same zone — and only electric_shower stays per-person. SHARED_WITHIN_EPISODE and PER_PERSON_APPLIANCES must between them cover every appliance in TRIGGERS, enforced by a raise

## Reason

I-027. Triggers fire per activity interval and the activity engine emits one interval per *person*, so a family of three sitting down to dinner boiled the kettle three times. Zone is part of the grouping because two people watching television together share one set and two watching in different rooms do not
