# D-057 — Chronotype selects the sleep-timing quantile rather than adding an offset of its own;

| | |

## Decision

Chronotype selects the sleep-timing quantile rather than adding an offset of its own; bedtime is anchored once, independently of each person's wake base

## Reason

Adding an hourly offset on top of a sourced distribution would widen the population's marginal past the source - which happened twice in building this: computing bedtime from the drawn wake gave it both spreads (10-90 range 389 minutes against the source's 187, median displaced 48 minutes, a tenth of the town pinned at the clip), and giving non-workers a later wake base pushed their bedtime 90 minutes later too, sending 23:00 to +0.227. Waking later means sleeping longer, not going to bed later, which is what HETUS implies anyway
