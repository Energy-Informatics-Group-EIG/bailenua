## Decision

Every declared JSON-stat source's own label is compared against the registry's declared title, with a threshold measured from the corpus

## Reason

I-053: two vital-statistics sources were declared under crossed matrices - births pointing at VSA09, which publishes deaths - and neither carried an age dimension at all. Nothing had ever opened them, because sweep flag A-25 scheduled their first use for week 2 step 5, so the registry's claim went untested for a week. A declared source that nothing consumes is an unverified claim. Across the 34 fetched cubes the lowest real content-word overlap is 0.40, where a title adds dimension breakdowns the CSO's short label omits; the two I-053 cases score 0.00. The threshold sits at 0.25, and a test asserts the guard still fails on the historical error
