# D-046 — A sweep search term should be the subject noun, not the phrase a reader expects;

## Decision

A sweep search term should be the subject noun, not the phrase a reader expects; the breadth guard exists to make that affordable

## Reason

A phrase tests one word order. cooking fuel and gas cooking both missed six tables that say "fuel used for cooking". The sweep already knew dimension labels matter more than titles - that is how HAS02 and HS023 were found - and now also knows the term itself has to be short. BROAD_TERM_LIMIT at 300 is what makes a plain term safe to use rather than something to avoid
