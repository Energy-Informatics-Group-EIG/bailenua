# D-133 — one master seed for the whole chain; a per-year seed is recorded but NOT fed to the model.

| | |
|---|---|
| Kind | implementation decision |
| Recorded in | `docs/DECISIONS.md replay section` |

## Decision

Instruction 1d asked for "seed per year derived deterministically from a master seed and recorded". Deriving a *different* seed per year and using it would break the thing the chain exists for: the population comes from synthesise_t0(master_seed), so a new seed each year would give a different town each year and destroy the cohort continuity that the whole emergent-outcomes framing (§1.1) depends on.
