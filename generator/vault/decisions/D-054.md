## Decision

The small hours belong to yesterday's drawn bedtime, recovered from the (person, sim_day - 1) stream rather than carried as state; the runner supplies yesterday's day type from the calendar

## Reason

I-044. place(0, wake, "sleep") claimed minutes 0 to wake unconditionally, so every bedtime past midnight was computed and discarded and nobody in the town was awake after midnight on any night. Re-drawing from the counter-based stream is exact, needs no state to cross the day boundary, and so leaves multi-worker determinism and checkpoint replay untouched - carrying a last-night field in HouseholdState would have put a behavioural quantity into the reproducibility contract for no gain. Yesterday's day type matters because a Friday night runs later than a Sunday one
