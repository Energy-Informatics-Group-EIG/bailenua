## Decision

The room thermostat lives inside the solver, proportioning on the indoor temperature over a 1.5 K band; _heating_schedule supplies only emitter availability and the setpoint, and the solver reports the heat actually delivered

## Reason

I-033. The controller proportioned on the OUTDOOR temperature and never looked at the room, so it overshot to 40 °C in nearly every month and could not let solar or internal gain displace any heating — 216 kWh against 52 on the same day. A thermostat has to see the room, and the room is what the solver computes, so the controller belongs there and nowhere else
