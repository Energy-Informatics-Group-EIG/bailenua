# D-066 — The 8.2 staleness margin is derived from LP1's measured maximum quarter-hour ramp (0.256 of mean), not from the model's own output

## Decision

The 8.2 staleness margin is derived from LP1's measured maximum quarter-hour ramp (0.256 of mean), not from the model's own output

## Reason

The margin's job is to cover how much a connection can move between network solves. Measuring that from our own series would have the model certifying itself - the contract's guarantee would be circular. LP1 is the settlement profile for an urban domestic connection, so its largest published quarter-hour ramp is a published per-connection ramp. It is an after-diversity ramp, so it is a LOWER bound on what one dwelling needs, and the coupling-lag test is written knowing that
