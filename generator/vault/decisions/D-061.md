# D-061 — The vacancy rate is an outcome, not a quota: the chain refills vacancies and never empties a home to hit a target

## Decision

The vacancy rate is an outcome, not a quota: the chain refills vacancies and never empties a home to hit a target

## Reason

VAC04 puts Cork County at 5 to 6 per cent and the slice starts fully occupied, so year one can only approach that from below. A selector that vacated homes to reach the published rate would be fabricating moves to satisfy a statistic. The test is therefore two claims - the rate never EXCEEDS the published maximum, and once the chain has run a year it sits INSIDE the published range - and the first version, which asserted the range from year one, failed correctly
