# D-129 — the circular-sourced school calendar is used for ALL years, 2022 included.

## Decision

The alternative was to keep SCHOOL_TERMS_2022 for 2022 so every existing artefact reproduced bit-for-bit, and use derived data only for other years. Rejected: it would make 2022 the one year whose school calendar came from a different and demonstrably wrong source (I-109, five days), and a replay whose claim is that the dates are real cannot carry a hand-built year in the middle.
