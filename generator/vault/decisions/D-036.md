## Decision

F2063 validates the rooms x dwelling-type joint and may not be used to tune ROOM_ALLOCATION_STRENGTH or DWELLING_SIZE_RANK

## Reason

Those constants were set before the sweep found F2063, so the table judges them: the model reproduces 85% of Cork County's detached-minus-apartment gradient (2.63 of 3.08 rooms) and sits within 0.29 rooms of every conditional mean. That is the first time the I-031 fix has been checked against external data rather than against a feasibility test. Fitting the constants to it would turn validation into calibration and the source's declaration would have to change with it (I-040)
