## Decision

Where a reference publishes overlapping categories, the model's value is checked against the feasible interval of their union, with the interval's width reported

## Reason

HBS refrigeration categories sum past 100% (Refrigerator 25.6%, Refrigerator with freezer 81.6%), so the share with ANY refrigerator - what the model's single prior means - is bounded, not published. A first version of this check scored 0.99 against 81.6% and failed, which was comparing a union against a part: a flaw in the check, not the model. Discarding the appliance would have been the other error; the valid inference is the interval, declared as weak (width 0.184) rather than presented as tight
