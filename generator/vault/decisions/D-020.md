# D-020 — Room counts are allocated to dwellings by size rank (dwelling type and bedrooms) with jitter, preserving the SAPS room marginal exactly;

## Decision

Room counts are allocated to dwellings by size rank (dwelling type and bedrooms) with jitter, preserving the SAPS room marginal exactly; MIN_ROOMS_OVER_BEDROOMS = 0, set by a feasibility test on the published marginals rather than assumed

## Reason

I-031. Independence between marginals is the right default, but it emitted eight-room apartments and five-bedroom dwellings with three rooms — not "no correlation" but violations of a logical constraint. The margin itself had to come from the data: at margin 2 the published room and bedroom marginals are jointly infeasible for 14.8% of dwellings even under the optimal pairing, so the constraint, not the data, was wrong
