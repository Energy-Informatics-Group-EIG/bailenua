## Decision

Wall, roof, floor and window U-values come from SEAI's assessed distribution conditional on (era cell, BER band); the BER-band multiplier is not also applied to them. Door, thermal bridging and infiltration keep the era table and stay [A]

## Reason

The multiplier is the mechanism I-048 showed to be wrong — capped at 0.55, it cannot reach a deeply retrofitted old house (floor 2.1 x 0.55 = 1.155 against an assessed 0.25) and it penalises a modern dwelling whose fabric meets the regulations whatever its band. Applying it on top of the assessed conditional would count the band twice, because the conditional already depends on it. The source publishes no door U-value, no bridging factor and no air-change rate, so those three keep the era table; derive_rc reports fabric_sourced_share_of_hlc, measured at 0.34 to 0.73, so the claim can be checked rather than taken on trust
