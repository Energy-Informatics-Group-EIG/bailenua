## Decision

The comfort-taking reference moves from 2.08 to 1.813 W/K/m², and this is not a new choice

## Reason

D-048 defines the reference as the town's median specific heat loss. Sourcing the fabric narrowed the town from 0.63–6.49 W/K/m² (factor 10.3, median 2.08) to 0.859–5.642 (factor 6.6, median 1.813), which is what I-048 predicted. So the median moved and the declared rule says the reference follows. test_the_reference_is_the_towns_median_not_its_mean caught it before anything was run — the constant was stale by 0.27 W/K/m² and the suite said so. The consequence for U31 is stated rather than hidden: the measured change is the fabric plus this entailed re-centring, and U31's comfort-off arm isolates the pure fabric effect at no extra cost, because the reference does not apply there
