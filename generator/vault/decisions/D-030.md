## Decision

The week 2 slice size is a separate named scope (week2_slice in config/e1_scope.yaml) and assert_within_e1_scope takes a scope_name; the E1 slice block is untouched at 50 homes

## Reason

U29 asked for 60 homes. Raising slice.n_homes would have raised the ceiling that every E1 artefact was produced and re-verified under, and the E1 gate's scope assertion would then no longer be the assertion that gate passed. Two scopes cost one parameter and keep E1 reproducible against exactly what E0-7 approved
