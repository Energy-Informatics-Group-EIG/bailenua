## Decision

The U23 mixed-age slice selects its seed small area by a declared objective (pre-1980 share, D–G share, BER-letter count, penalised for departing from the town's household size) and then searches the seed areas for one whose slice also satisfies E0-7's people band and the pre-1980 floor. The band is a hard constraint, never a term in the objective. slice_kind defaults to "e1_estate" so every E1 artefact reproduces unchanged

## Reason

The first selector made the people band a penalty weighted 0.8 against an age reward of 1.0, and the best-scoring seed gave 112 people against a 115 lower bound — three short. Re-weighting until it fitted would have been choosing the weights to get the answer; searching the seeds is the same work done honestly, and it raises U23Infeasible when no seed satisfies both
