## Decision

The minutes a shower's water runs are separate from the length of the shower_bath episode, capped by it, and declared

## Reason

Charging 6 L/min across the whole episode - time in the bathroom, up to 25 minutes - made 150-litre showers and about 800 litres a day per household, 70 to 85% of all hot water. DEAP publishes the flow rate and not the duration, so the duration is a declared assumption; PL-DHW-01 bounds its consequence against DEAP's own 125 L/person/day all-water target rather than leaving it unchecked
