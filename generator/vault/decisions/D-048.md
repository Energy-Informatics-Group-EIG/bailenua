# D-048 — The mechanism is centred on the town's median specific heat loss, not its mean

## Decision

The mechanism is centred on the town's median specific heat loss, not its mean

## Reason

The distribution is right-skewed - 0.63 to 6.49 W/K/m2, median 2.08 against mean 2.46 - so centring on the mean would put most dwellings on the rebound side and give the mechanism a net warming effect on the town instead of a redistribution across the fabric range. Comfort-taking redistributes; a test pins that the town-mean shift stays small against the spread it creates
