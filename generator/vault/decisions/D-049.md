## Decision

The comfort-taking strength is not fitted to DBERNG01, and the sweep is published instead

## Reason

DBERNG01 is the declared calibration axis so fitting would have been permitted. But the mechanism saturates at 45% spread against a 4-7% target, so a fitted strength would buy nothing except the appearance of calibration while costing a kelvin of indoor temperature. The declared amplitudes stand and the sweep sits beside them so a reader can see what each strength costs
