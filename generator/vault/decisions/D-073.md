## Decision

Pre-registered before running U31. Under the sourced BER fabric the old/new gas gradient must fall from 1.6158; the old-stock at-meter ratio must fall from 1.1806 and the new-stock ratio must rise from 0.7307, each paired-significant at p < 0.05; both must move towards 1.0; and the gradient must not fall below 1.0. Expected magnitude, stated but not a pass condition: gradient ≈ 1.21

## Reason

Written and committed before the run, because the fabric was derived from a source (SEAI BER Research Tool) that is now tuning-exposed, and U31 is the only independent evidence that the change earns its keep. A criterion chosen after seeing the gradient would be worthless. The magnitude is derived from the fabric alone and never from U31: the envelope heat-loss coefficient moves by x0.924 on the old group and x1.657 on the new (measured on the held-out slice's own 60 dwellings), and space heat is 58.8% of old-group meter gas and 41.5% of new-group (heldout_u31.json), which gives 1.1806 x 0.955 = 1.13 and 0.7307 x 1.273 = 0.93, hence 1.21.
