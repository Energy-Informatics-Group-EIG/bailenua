## Decision

The two groups are balanced in size (30 and 30) and the slice is checked to be balanced on floor area before use

## Reason

The difference of two means has its standard error dominated by the smaller cell, so equal allocation minimises it. And DBERNG01's actual finding is that measured consumption tracks dwelling SIZE - so a size-confounded slice could not separate fabric from size, and any gradient on it would be partly a size effect wearing a fabric label. Measured: Welch p = 0.754 on floor area (balanced) and p = 3.5e-08 on specific heat loss (contrasted, 1.82x)
