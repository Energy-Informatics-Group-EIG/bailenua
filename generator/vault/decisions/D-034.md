## Decision

A verdict of RETIRED requires the source declared, fetched and scored against; a source found but not yet used is SOURCE_FOUND and must name the step that will use it

## Reason

An unused source retires nothing - that is precisely what happened to HAS02 for four days. The distinction is enforced in AssumptionFlag.__post_init__, so the sweep cannot claim a retirement it has not made
