# D-067 — The conservative envelope is the minimum of the connection's headroom and its share of the upstream element's, not the connection's alone

## Decision

The conservative envelope is the minimum of the connection's headroom and its share of the upstream element's, not the connection's alone

## Reason

A literal reading of 8.2's wording gave per-connection headroom only, and twenty homes each inside their own 12 kW service limit collectively overloaded a 40 kW feeder. C1 then extracted 1,213 kWh against C2's 908 while causing the violations, because a 15-minute commitment is made before a 15-minute-old solve can reveal the overload - which is exactly the gain from stale information the contract forbids. It appears only when the binding element is upstream, so a test at one operating point would have certified a broken contract. The equal share is conservative and is a choice; a real allocation is a network calculation, which is week 3
