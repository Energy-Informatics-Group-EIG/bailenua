## Decision

The cooking-fuel share is derived from HEBEU34 and HEBEU106 rather than declared, and assigned three ways: all-gas, gas hob with electric oven, all-electric

## Reason

U30's phrase search for cooking fuel found nothing and was wrong - the CSO's tables are titled "Main fuel used for cooking in households" (I-046). The share conditional on a gas connection is P(cooks with gas) / P(gas connected) = 14%/48% and 9%/48%, both from the same survey and year so the ratio does not straddle two definitions. The "combination" category is not a residual: it is a gas hob with an electric oven, and the model already priced hob and oven separately.
