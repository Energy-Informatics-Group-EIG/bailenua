## Decision

Comfort-taking is three COM-B terms with different drivers, not one amplitude

## Reason

A single knob fitted to DBERNG01's flatness would reproduce the flatness and tell us nothing. Three terms can disagree with the data, and the falsifiable consequence is that prebound is a COST response: the same leaky dwelling takes -1.50 K from a quintile-1 local-authority household and -0.00 K from a well-off owner. No fabric correction can produce that
