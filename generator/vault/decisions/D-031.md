## Decision

The slice's people count is tested to equal the sum of its households' actual sizes (test_household_sizes_are_taken_as_the_data_give_them)

## Reason

U29's "keep household sizes as the data give them" needs an assertion, not an intention. Without it, a future selector could meet the people band by adjusting a household rather than by choosing which part of the town to simulate — and that is fabricating population to satisfy a scope constraint
