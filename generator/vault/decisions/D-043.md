## Decision

Hot water DEMAND is built from draw events tied to simulated activities, not from DEAP's floor-area route, and DEAP's figure is kept as the thing the result is judged against

## Reason

U25 asks for draw events, and the activity engine already simulates showering, bathing, washing up and cooking with HETUS durations. Deriving demand from floor area the way DEAP does would make the model reproduce the reference by construction and unable to disagree with it. The shower volume is the part that is not assumed: DEAP's 6 L/min minimum times the minutes the water runs
