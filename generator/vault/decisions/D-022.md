# D-022 — The flatness of measured gas consumption across BER bands is treated as a finding to report, not a target to fit.

## Decision

The flatness of measured gas consumption across BER bands is treated as a finding to report, not a target to fit. The physics keeps its fixed setpoint and schedule at E1

## Reason

DBERNG01 shows gas use varying only 4–7% across BER bands within a dwelling type, and A–B detached houses using *more* gas than F–G, while varying 84% across dwelling types. That is comfort-taking, a behavioural mechanism design 5.3 locates in COM-B and which E1 deliberately does not wire into the thermostat. Fitting the physics to reproduce a behavioural effect would hide the gap instead of measuring it, and would make the fabric parameters wrong for every other purpose
