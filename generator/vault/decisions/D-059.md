## Decision

The hazard integrated at draw time is recorded and compared against the exposure-based expectation, every run

## Reason

The VSA36 parity reweighting multiplied each woman's rate by a parity share, which rescaled every rate downwards and lost 12% of the births: 10.15 integrated against an exposure expectation of 11.52. From the outside that is indistinguishable from a model missing its target, and no vital-statistics interval would have caught it - 10.15 was comfortably inside the CI. The fix normalises the parity weights to mean 1.0 within each band so the published band rate is preserved exactly, and the comparison stays as a permanent check because the bug class is invisible without it
