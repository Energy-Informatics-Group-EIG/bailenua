## Decision

A counterfactual refuses a household-scoped fork, and paired and unpaired entities are reported separately rather than pooled

## Reason

Design 8.2's explicit rule is that any intervention forces a town fork, and a single-household replay is cheap and tempting and would be wrong invisibly: the home's own load would respond correctly while the feeder, the venues and the later events stayed at baseline, so the estimate would miss exactly the coupled effect the intervention was interesting for. On CRN, the counter-based tree makes the same coordinate give the same number for free, so what is actually measured is whether the same coordinates are VISITED - and an entity the intervention does not touch that decouples anyway is a finding, because a paired statistic over it compares two random sequences while reporting the variance …
