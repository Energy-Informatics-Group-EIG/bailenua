## Decision

PL-PEAK-01's load-factor band is asymmetric: at most 0.05 above LP1's, and at most 0.15 below only once 1,000 homes are aggregated

## Reason

Both the diversity and the resolution arguments push the measured value down - a town of 1,100 is spikier than two million meters, and a peak measured over a shorter interval cannot be lower. Exceeding the national class is therefore not a tolerance question but a sign that load has been spread too evenly, which is one of the tells 7.9 audits for. The E1 slice's 0.2565 against LP1's 0.4129 is the expected direction and size for 50 homes, not a fault
