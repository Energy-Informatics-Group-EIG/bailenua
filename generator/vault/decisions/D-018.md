## Decision

The E1 estate's seed small area is chosen as the one whose own mean household size is closest to the town's, among areas holding at least 60% of the estate, and is then filled from its nearest neighbours by OSi centroid distance; order *within* an area is a seeded permutation

## Reason

E0-7 fixes both 50 homes and about 140 people, which is 2.8 per home against the town's 2.671, while Cobh's small areas genuinely range from about 2.0 to 3.4. Most contiguous blocks of 50 therefore miss 140 badly. This is a selection on the outcome and is declared as such: the alternative buys spatial tidiness at the cost of a slice that is not a miniature of the town, and E1 exists to exercise the engine on a miniature of the town. The permutation matters separately — building_id order is household-size descending, so "the first 40 of 44" silently takes the forty biggest households, which is what returned 176 people
