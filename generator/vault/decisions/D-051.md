## Decision

U31's held-out slice is stratified and not contiguous, gas-only, and not people-band constrained, each departure declared in its diagnostics

## Reason

D-018 and D-028 selected contiguous blocks because R01 wants estates and 6.4 wants a sub-metered subset sharing a feeder. No feeder study runs on this slice, and Cobh's pre-1980 and post-2005 stock are in different parts of the town, so contiguity would cost exactly the coverage the comparison needs. DBERNG02 is a gas meter reading, so a dwelling burning no gas contributes nothing. E0-7's people band sizes the SIMULATED slice; this one's constraint is enough dwellings per cell to take a mean, and forcing the band would fight the stratification
