## Decision

Solar gain is computed from the irradiance incident on the glazing, transposed onto vertical planes at the four cardinal orientations with an even glazing split; purge ventilation is closed-loop inside the solver, gated on outside being cooler and on no heat being delivered

## Reason

I-032. aperture × GHI applied a horizontal-surface value to vertical windows and had the wrong sign in both seasons, and the F1 network had no heat-removal path at all, so the annual median living temperature was 24.6 °C against an Irish 17–19. The even orientation split is DEAP's own assumption for a dwelling of unknown orientation and the conservative one for overheating; real orientations are available from the OSi footprints in weeks 2–3
