# D-068 — Envelope tightening and relaxation are rates per unit of time, not per epoch

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-10-10 |
| Kind | implementation decision |
| Reversible | Yes |
| Recorded in | `docs/DECISIONS.md table` |

## Decision

Envelope tightening and relaxation are rates per unit of time, not per epoch

## Reason

Tightening by a fixed factor per violating epoch made C2, with 1440 epochs a day against C1's 96, tighten up to fifteen times as hard for the same physical violations. The two coupling modes would then have differed because of epoch counting rather than because of staleness, which is precisely the confound that makes a coupling-lag test meaningless. Violations are reported in MINUTES for the same reason: a quarter-hour overload is one row at C1 and fifteen at C2
