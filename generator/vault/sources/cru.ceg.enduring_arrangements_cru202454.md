## Used for

D27's export rate. The CRU records that export tariffs offered by suppliers 'have generally ranged from circa 16c/kWh to 25c/kWh' and that the floor price of zero for flat-rate export tariffs is retained. So the EXISTENCE of an export payment, its published RANGE and its regulated FLOOR are all sourced; the single number on the card is the midpoint of the published range, by a stated rule. This replaces the DEAP manual's 'currently 0c', which predates the guarantee. Exposure `none`.
